A Trusted Expert in Three Domains

I'm Marquel Smith, a seasoned professional based in Texas, USA, with over six years of hands-on experience across IT consulting, academic writing, and AI data annotation. My work spans continents and industries — from Silicon Valley startups to European research institutions.

In the AI space, I specialize in data annotation, dataset labeling, content moderation, and training data curation — helping machine learning teams build more accurate models, faster.
